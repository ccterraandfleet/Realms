# Motion provenance

## Combat

The current four attack clips derive from Carnegie Mellon University's Graphics Lab motion-capture database, subject 02, trials 07 and 08 (swordplay).

- https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/search.php?subjectnumber=2&motion=sword
- https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/02/02.asf
- https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/02/02_07.amc
- https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/02/02_08.amc

Attribution: CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217. Consult https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/ for source usage terms.

ASF/AMC bone-axis corrections are applied, and motion is sampled from 120 to 30 fps. Left cut, right cut, overhead and forward extension use separate recorded segments. The thrust extension is rotated about the shoulder toward gameplay aim, preserving limb lengths. Blade direction is adapted from wrist orientation and attack aim, not captured prop tracking. Playback follows the combat phases.

## Locomotion

Walk, run and sprint derive from Ubisoft LAFAN1, subject 2: https://github.com/ubisoft/ubisoft-laforge-animation-dataset . Review its licence before commercial distribution. This prototype is not claimed to be cleared for commercial release.

The corrected BVH parser keeps terminal-joint offsets separate from End Site offsets. Root travel determines stride speed, loops close over a whole gait cycle, and a common gait phase aligns blends. Runtime IK anchors planted toes and preserves knee bend during strafing and backpedalling.

## Exchange and contact revision

The live duel now samples rendered blade paths throughout release, rather than awarding damage on entering release. `combat-traces.json` is generated by `chiv/tests/bake-contact.mjs` from the same body rig, capturedMotion function, first-person targetPose adaptation and arm-reach solver used for rendering. Runtime sweeps test a character capsule in world space, incorporating facing and player look pitch. Weapon aim warping preserves captured arm movement while pointing the blade into the intended strike line.

The behavioural reference for guard attacks, matching counters, ripostes, heavy commitment and attack-to-attack feints is the community guide “So You've Skipped the Tutorial”: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2948723102 . This is a community reference, not official source code. The weapon phase and damage rows remain the existing community datatable import.

Prototype values not established by the imported weapon table include capsule dimensions, guard drain, counter stamina refund, active-parry duration and the brief post-block defence lock. They remain tuning parameters, not a claim of exact Chivalry 2 numerical parity. Current world combat is still a duel prototype; a single attack cleaving several independently simulated combatants and production network reconciliation remain outside this release.


## Physical dodge and action integrity

The 2026-11-15 follow-up found that dodge previously changed a rules state without moving either body. Movement now runs on fixed combat steps, and dodge translates the same physical positions used for blade collision. Chivalry-mode dodge no longer grants invisible immunity when a blade actually contacts the body. Both combatants use the same movement contract.

`Default.Dash.Cooldown = 1.0` and per-ability cooldowns come from the imported AbilitiesOverride rows. Paid abilities reject insufficient stamina instead of silently clamping it to zero. Dodge cannot restart itself or escape stagger. Cancelling or interrupting an attack clears its stale active-parry/riposte protection. Matching counters apply only to slash, overhead and stab types.

The dodge distance (1.55 m), duration (0.42 s), and unspecified stamina values remain prototype parameters, not measured reference-game values. The import leaves Dash stamina unset. This correction closes reproducible control and state-integrity defects; it does not establish complete Chivalry numerical parity.
