Hold T to bite into clear ground. Release early for a smaller scoop. Carry it, face a drop spot, then hold T again to tip.

WASD / mouse
Move and look. Click the world to capture the mouse.
Left click
Attack. Latest mouse motion chooses left/right cut, overhead or thrust.
Hold left click
Charge the committed attack into a heavy.
Right mouse
Hold guard. Attack from guard with the matching line to attempt a counter.
J / K
Jab / kick. A kick punishes held guard.
Space
Dodge. A/D chooses side; otherwise step back.
Shift
Sprint. Position matters; damage follows blade contact.
E / G / 1
Pick up nearby cargo or a weapon, set cargo down, or equip at your armoury.
V
Switch between first-person and third-person views.
E / Y near your horse
Mount or dismount when stopped. Feed at the home stable using House grain.
While riding
W/S ride, brake or reverse. A/D steer continuously; mouse looks around. Shift gallops. Dismount before fighting or handling cargo.
F / Tab / M
Toggle an assisted work shift, open House affairs, or the command map.
Escape
Release the mouse. The living world does not pause.

Changing quality saves the local rehearsal before reloading. In shared mode you reconnect to the same world. Nearby combat dismounts travel riders; mounted combat is not enabled.
